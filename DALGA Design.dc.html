About the atelier

Dalga means wave.

No wave breaks twice the same way. That is the idea DALGA DESIGN is built on — a small atelier where garments are not produced, but grown around one person at a time.

There is no collection calendar, no sizes, no restock. Each commission starts as a conversation and ends as a garment with exactly one owner. The pattern is archived and never cut again.

We work in wool, linen and raw cotton — fabrics that age with the person wearing them. Everything is cut, sewn and finished by hand in the atelier.

“A garment can be a portrait — of a person, a moment, a life about to change.”

No seasons

We make when you commission — not when a calendar says so. There is nothing to buy here, only something to begin.

No sizes

Every pattern starts from your measurements, your posture, your way of moving. Nothing on a rack, nothing standard.

No repeats

The day your piece is handed over, its pattern is archived. It will never be cut again — for anyone.

A signature,
not a label

The wave mark is pressed into every finished piece — the only place you will find our name. The rest of the garment speaks about you, not about us.

One of one

A DALGA commission is a slow, personal process. Everything you need to know before your piece begins.

01

Conversation

You write to us. We talk about the occasion, your style, the story the piece should carry. No commitment yet — just a conversation.

02

Concept & quote

We sketch the silhouette, propose fabrics and give you an individual quote. A deposit reserves your place in the season's calendar.

03

Fittings

Two to three sessions at the atelier. Not local? We work with a measurement guide and video fittings, with one final fitting in person whenever possible.

04

Hand-off

Six to ten weeks after the first conversation, your piece is finished by hand, pressed with the wave mark and handed over. The pattern is archived forever.

Good to know

Timeline

Six to ten weeks for most pieces. Occasion garments — weddings, premieres, milestones — should be commissioned at least three months ahead.

Investment

Quoted individually after the first conversation — based on fabric, construction and hours of handwork. Half on commission, half on hand-off.

Materials

Wool, linen and raw cotton by default; we can also work with a fabric that already means something to you — a scarf, a bolt, an heirloom.

The promise

Your pattern is never reused — not in another size, not in another fabric, not for anyone else. One garment, one owner.
